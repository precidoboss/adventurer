Game Modes
⚡The Gauntlet (Chapter II) — 5 hand-crafted stages of traps, moving platforms & Void Wardens. You have 3 lives per stage. Hit a trap = lose 1 life. Lose all 3 = restart that stage. Complete all 5 to win. Choose Knight or Ninja before entering.
⚔Endless Mode — Survive as long as possible. No time limit. Fight until death.
✦Shadow Chronicle — Complete 8 objectives in 3 mins. Speak to NPCs. Discover the password. Reach the boss.
🗝In Story Mode: Press F to interact with NPCs and the dimensional portal.
📋Press TAB in Story Mode to toggle the Objective Board.
⚡ Gauntlet — Tips & Traps
🔥Fire Pits — Step on them and you lose a life instantly. Always jump over.
⚔Spike Traps — Floor hazards. Walk around or jump clean.
➡Arrow Launchers — Shoot projectiles from the right. Watch the timing and dash through gaps.
🪨Falling Rocks — A ⚠ warning appears before each boulder drops. Move out from under the shadow.
🛡Knight Shield — The Knight absorbs your very first trap hit for free each stage. Use that safety net wisely.
💨Ninja Double Jump — Press W again in the air. Essential for crossing wide gaps on pendulum platforms.

Ancient guardian of each stage. Patrols its platform — stops at the edge and won't fall off. Attacks when you're at the same level and within reach. Hit it with your dash.

Ancient terror of the void. Attacks from range and melee in Story Mode. Enrages at 50% HP. Strike from the RIGHT for double damage if you know its secret. Your dragon companion can help wear it down.

✨ Artifacts — Found in Chests (Endless & Story)
[1] Iron Helm — +1 Max HP per pickup, applies instantly
[2] Shadow Berries — Instantly restores 5 HP on pickup
[3] Mana Crystal — Permanently increases sword damage by +1
[4] Fire Feather — Press 4 to activate fire shield for 10 seconds
[5] Ancient Core — A mysterious relic... reserved for the Boss encounter
Artifacts glow and rise from opened chests. Passive ones (1–3) apply instantly on pickup. Active ones (4–5) go to your hotbar — press the matching number key to use. Artifacts do NOT appear in The Gauntlet.
